The app is for people who wants to play pokemon competivily where it contains data of all the pokemon as of April,2026.
users can find the pokemon of their choice which will then reveal a line graph of the pokemon's total usage for each month.
With the graph, users can clic on each node that displays the info on the pokemon, what the most popular item, moves and ability to have at the time.
On hover over the moves will display what they do other than damage, they are color coded base on what element the move is and nexxt to them is the move category that have keys add the top to better understand what they do.

Clickables --
1. Name - displays the pokemon stats, typing and avalible abilities.
2. Teammates - opens up a new panel for the said team mate pokemon.
3. Arrow under the Pokemon picture - changes the panel to display a overview of how to play the pokemon, a guide.
